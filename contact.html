Common questions

Can I ask you to recommend a product for my room?

Yes — send the room, the rough budget and one photo if you have it. We answer as many as we can, and the best ones become guides.

Do you accept guest posts?

No. All guides are written in-house. We do consider original, unpublished photography collaborations.

I found a broken link or a wrong price. Where do I report it?

Email us with the page name. Prices move constantly, so corrections are genuinely helpful and we fix them quickly.

How do I get my product considered?

Email a one-paragraph pitch with a link and a price. We buy most of what we feature. If a product is sent for review it is disclosed in the post.
