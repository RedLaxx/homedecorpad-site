How this site works

Most decor content is aimed at people with a renovation budget. This site is built for the opposite: renters, first apartments, and anyone furnishing a room with a number in mind.

  • Free fixes before purchases. Layout, light and editing come first because they change a room the most and cost the least.
  • Prices and order of operations. We tell you what to buy first, so you never spend your budget on the wrong piece.
  • Honest about the cheap stuff. We will tell you when a budget item is not worth it — and what we would return.

The four-step method we use in every room

  1. Edit. Clear every surface, then put back only what earns its place. Free, and it does more than any purchase.
  2. Light it. Warm bulbs (2700K), three sources at three heights, and a dimmer wherever it is possible. Under $40 for most rooms.
  3. Anchor it. One large-size rug, and a colour palette of a dominant neutral plus one accent. This is where a room stops feeling like a rental.
  4. Layer it. Texture where colour would be busy: bouclé, linen, wood, brass, a basket, one plant. Never more than three objects per surface.

The rule behind all of it

Spend on the things your eye lands on — the rug, the lighting, the bedding — and save on the things it does not. A $25 throw in the right texture beats a $200 lamp you cannot see.
