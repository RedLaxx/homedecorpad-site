Wall Decor Ideas: 5 Styles to Try, From Kids' Jungle Rooms to Modern Minimal

Blank walls are the hardest part of decorating a room — and one of the cheapest to change. These five wall decor styles, from kids' jungle rooms to modern minimal, each come with a full guide, plus the hanging rules and mistakes that apply to all of them.

Wall Decor & Gallery Walls · HomeDecorPad

Blank walls are the hardest part of decorating a room. Furniture is easy, since it goes on the floor and does its job. A wall asks you to decide what the room is about, and a common response is to hang one small frame in the middle and hope for the best.

Walls are also one of the cheaper ways to change how a room feels. A few pieces can make a room calmer, warmer or more grown-up, and most of them work in a rental without a drill.

We've written five wall decor guides, one for each style. Read the one that fits your room, or go through all five and borrow from each.

How to use these guides

Pick the style closest to your room, open that guide, and choose one wall to start with. One finished wall looks better than four half-done ones. Each guide is a list of ideas, so save your favorites to a Pinterest board and compare them before you buy anything. This week, try one change: hang a single large piece, add a woven basket or swap a frame.

The five styles

1. Kids' jungle wall decor

Kids Jungle Wall Decor: 15 Ideas for a Nursery or Playroom

Jungle is a fun theme for a child's room, and the walls carry most of it. This guide covers murals, removable decals, framed animal prints, a small gallery wall and woven baskets, and how to keep the room calm enough for a child to sleep in.

2. Bohemian wall decor

Bohemian Wall Decor: 17 Ideas With Macramé, Baskets and Earthy Textures

Boho walls are layered and look handmade. This guide covers macramé, woven baskets and tapestries in terracotta, sage and warm cream, and how to hang a collected wall so it looks deliberate rather than messy.

3. Feminine wall decor

Feminine Wall Decor: 14 Soft, Elegant Ideas for Bedrooms and Living Rooms

This guide uses gentle colors, florals, curved mirrors and pretty frames to make a bedroom or living room feel elegant without going overboard.

4. Cottage wall decor

Cottage Wall Decor: 15 Cozy Ideas for a Soft Modern Cottage Look

Cottage style is warm, a little nostalgic and full of character. This guide has ideas for florals, vintage-style frames, plates and shelves, with enough of a modern edge that the room doesn't look dated.

5. Modern wall decor

Modern Wall Decor: 15 Ideas Beyond the Gallery Wall

This guide goes beyond the standard grid of frames. It covers oversized art, textured finishes and natural materials, and how to keep a minimal room warm.

Wall decor rules that work in every style

These basics make wall decor look finished in any style.

  • Center art at eye level, roughly 57 inches (145 cm) from the floor, and hang it close enough to the furniture below that the two read as a group.
  • Go bigger. One large piece usually looks better than several small ones scattered across a wall.
  • Leave empty wall space around what you hang, so each piece looks chosen.
  • Pull two or three colors from the room into your art, frames and textiles.
  • In a rental, lean art on a shelf or console, or use removable hooks, peel-and-stick wallpaper or tension rods — our renter-friendly wall decor guide covers the damage-free versions in detail.

Common wall decor mistakes

  • Hanging art too high. It's an easy mistake, and it disconnects the art from the room.
  • Choosing art that's too small. A tiny frame above a large sofa looks lost; aim for about two-thirds the width of the furniture beneath it.
  • Buying everything at once. Hang one piece, live with it for a week, then add more.
  • Ignoring the light. Check how a wall looks in the morning and evening before you commit to a color or finish.
