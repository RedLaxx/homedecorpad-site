The Boho Bedroom Board: 22 IKEA & Amazon Finds for a Warm, Layered Space

This is a full boho bedroom moodboard built from 22 real pieces from IKEA and Amazon — a rattan bed frame, sage tufted bedding, macramé, jute rugs, warm paper lighting, and the small finishing touches that make a room feel lived in. Every product below is one you can click through and buy today, with prices for both US and UK readers.

Start with the palette, steal the four styling rules, then shop the pieces that fit your budget. Whether you are starting from a blank room or just swapping out bedding and wall art, those three steps in that order are the whole method.

The target look: a woven rattan headboard, macramé on the wall, a big paper pendant, and layered neutral textiles. Photo: IKEA.

Boho isn't a colour — it's a texture. Get the materials right and the room works with almost no decoration at all.

The colour palette

Boho rooms work because of texture, not bold colour. Keep the walls soft and warm, then layer natural materials. If you want one accent, make it terracotta — not navy, not black.

  • Warm white #F4EDE1 — walls and ceiling
  • Sand #E9DFCE — curtains and the bedding base
  • Rattan #C2A075 — furniture and frames
  • Terracotta #B96A4B — accent only, about 10%
  • Sage #8A9A7B — plants and one textile
  • Espresso #332F2B — lamp bases and furniture legs
  1. One wood tone, repeated. Pick a honey or rattan wood and repeat it in the bed,
  2. nightstands, mirror frame and shelves. Mixing five different woods is the single fastest way to make a boho room look cluttered instead of collected.
  3. Texture over pattern. Tufting, woven cotton, jute, macramé, cane webbing. Two
  4. patterned pieces maximum — everything else should be something you want to touch.
  5. 60 / 30 / 10. Sixty percent warm neutrals, thirty percent rattan, wood and jute, ten
  6. percent terracotta or sage. That final ten percent is where the room gets its personality.
  7. Odd numbers, varied heights. Group objects in threes and fives at different heights —
  8. tall pampas, a mid-size plant, a short candle. Perfect symmetry reads as hotel room; boho needs a little asymmetry.

The one rule that does the most work

Texture over pattern. Boho fails the moment it turns into a print competition — tufted cotton, woven jute, cane webbing and macramé give you depth that no amount of pattern can.

The bed

The bed is the room. A woven or cane headboard does about 80% of the boho work on its own — everything else just has to agree with it.

Lighting: the mood maker

No overhead lights after dark. You want three light sources at knee-to-shoulder height: a pendant over each nightstand (or one big paper globe), plus a table lamp. This is the section people under-buy, and it is the one that decides whether your bedroom feels like a retreat or a rental.

How it all goes together

A rough top-down plan for a standard bedroom. The three things that matter:

  • Centre the bed on the main wall. Not on the wall with the window — the wall you see

when you walk in. That is what makes the room feel deliberate rather than accidental.

  • Run the rug out from under the bed. The rug should extend 18–24 inches past the bed on

the sides and foot. A rug that stops at the bed frame makes the whole room look small.

  • Put the mirror opposite the window. It bounces daylight back into the room and doubles

whatever light you have. Lean it against the wall rather than hanging it.

Everything else — the nightstands, the dresser, the plants — goes around those three decisions. Get them right and the rest is just arranging.

If you only buy five things

A rattan or wicker bed frame, a tufted duvet set, a macramé wall hanging, a jute rug one size up, and a paper or rattan pendant. Those five do about 90% of the work — everything else on this list is refinement.

The takeaway

A boho bedroom isn't a shopping list of random pretty things — it is one wood tone, a lot of texture, and light you can dim. Start with the bed frame and the bedding, live with it for a month, then add the wall art and the plants. The room will end up looking like it came together slowly, which is exactly the point.

If you do one thing today, make it the macramé above the bed and the pendant light. Those two pieces change the feeling of a bedroom more than anything else on this list.
