## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your computer or mobile device when you visit. They let the site remember your actions and preferences (such as your cookie choices) and help site owners, advertisers, and analytics providers understand how the site is used. We also use similar technologies — pixels (tiny images), tags, scripts, and browser local storage — which work in comparable ways. In this Policy we call all of these "cookies."

Cookies may be **first-party** (set by the Site) or **third-party** (set by another company whose content or service appears on the Site). They may be **session** cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or **persistent** cookies (stored until they expire or you delete them).

## 4. How to manage cookies

**Consent banner / Privacy Settings.** If you are in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, you will see a banner on your first visit where you can accept, reject, or customize non-essential cookies. You can change your choices at any time via the **"Cookie Settings" / "Privacy Settings"** link in the Site footer.

**Do Not Sell or Share (California and other U.S. states).** Use the **"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"** link in the footer to opt out of the sharing of your information for targeted advertising. We also honor **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** browser signals where required.

**Browser settings.** Most browsers let you view, delete, and block cookies:
- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies / Site data
- Safari: Preferences → Privacy
- Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security
- Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
Blocking all cookies may stop parts of the Site from working properly.

**Opt-out tools for interest-based advertising:**
- Google: [Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads)
- Digital Advertising Alliance (US): [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices)
- Network Advertising Initiative (US): [optout.networkadvertising.org](https://optout.networkadvertising.org/)
- Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada: [youradchoices.ca](https://youradchoices.ca/)
- European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance: [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/)
- Google Analytics: [opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- Pinterest: your Pinterest account → *Settings → Privacy and data*

Opt-outs generally rely on cookies, so if you clear your cookies or use a different browser or device, you may need to opt out again.

**Mobile devices.** You can limit ad tracking in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
