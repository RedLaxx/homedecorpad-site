## 3. How affiliate links work

When you click an affiliate link, the retailer or network places a small tracking cookie on your device. If you make a qualifying purchase within the cookie's window (for example, 24 hours for Amazon, longer for many other programs), the retailer pays us a percentage of the sale. **You pay exactly the same price** whether you use our link or go to the retailer directly. We never see your name, address, or payment details — only aggregated sales and commission reports.

In some cases, an affiliate link may also earn us a commission on other items you add to your cart during the same session, not just the item we linked to.
