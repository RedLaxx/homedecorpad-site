About HomeDecorPad

HomeDecorPad is a home decor site for people who want a room that looks considered — on a normal budget, in a normal apartment, without a renovation.

Why this site exists

Interior design content usually shows you the after photo and hides the invoice. We do the opposite. Every guide includes the price ranges, the order to buy things in, and the things we would not spend money on.

The site started in 2026 as a way to organise our own decorating notes: which purchases changed a room, which ones photographed well but annoyed us within a month, and which "designer" looks could be recreated with a shopping list instead of a contractor.

Who it is for

  • Renters who cannot drill, paint or replace anything.
  • First-apartment and small-space dwellers who need every purchase to earn its footprint.
  • Anyone who wants a warm, calm, lived-in home rather than a showroom.

How we choose what to recommend

  1. Texture and warmth first. Matte finishes, natural materials and warm tones. We avoid glossy, printed and cold-toned decor even when it is popular.
  2. Scale matters as much as style. An undersized rug or a tiny lamp is the most common expensive mistake, so we recommend sizes before products.
  3. Real prices. We list typical street prices, and we say when something is only worth buying on sale.
  4. No paid placements. Brands cannot buy a mention, a ranking or a positive review. If we ever publish a sponsored post, it is labelled at the top of the page.

How HomeDecorPad makes money

Two ways only: display advertising, and affiliate commission when you buy through one of our links — at no extra cost to you. Both are explained in our Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure. Affiliate income never changes what we recommend.

Our editorial standards

  • We test or own the products we feature wherever possible. When we have not used something ourselves, we say so.
  • Illustrations on this site are original artwork created for HomeDecorPad. We do not publish other people's photographs as our own.
  • We correct mistakes. If a price, size or recommendation changes, the guide is updated and the date on the page changes with it.
  • Some images are AI-assisted. Where a graphic is generated rather than photographed by us, it is decorative only — it is never used to misrepresent a product you could buy.

Where we are

HomeDecorPad is written and run by a small team based in Lagos, Nigeria, for readers in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia. We link to retailers that ship to those countries and we price our recommendations in dollars.

Work with us

We partner with brands on sponsored placements, product features and affiliate campaigns — always labelled, always relevant to a budget-conscious reader. Email hello@homedecorpad.com or use the contact page and tell us what you have in mind.
